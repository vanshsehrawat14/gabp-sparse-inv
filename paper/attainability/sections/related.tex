\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{The exact engine.}
The Takahashi and Erisman--Tinney recurrences are classical
\citep{takahashi1973,erismantinney1975}; SelInv, PEXSI, and PSelInv extend
selected inversion through sparse elimination trees
\citep{lin2011selinv,lin2013pexsi,jacquelin2018pselinv}. Differentiation of
sparse solves and selected inverses is also well studied: it appears in sparse
log-determinants, TMB, and linear-time
reverse-mode banded operators for TensorFlow and PyTorch, where the sparse
inverse subset itself is differentiated at the forward operator's complexity
class \citep{zhu2019sparse,kristensen2016tmb,durrande2019banded}. Schwarz--Schur,
\texttt{torch-sla}, and JAX-AMG provide broader differentiable sparse-solver
surfaces; ADELIA provides an adjacent, structure-specialized inference
differentiation pipeline
\citep{wang2025schwarz,torchsla2026,boudaoud2026adelia,liu2026jaxamg}. The
implementation here is enabling machinery, not the scientific novelty.

\paragraph{Comparable fit.}
Comparable-loss comparisons already appear in
\citet{mueller2025trajectories,boursier2025simplicity}, and credit-assignment
rules that depart from the true gradient are reported to generalize worse and
more variably than rules that track it \citep{liu2022generalization}. Here the
gate is preregistered for exact-versus-finite numerical computation, and the causal
verdict stays negative when it does not open.

\paragraph{Source-generated access and nonlocal alternatives.}
Polynomial graph filters are classically hop-local
\citep{defferrard2016convolutional}, with newer measures of long-range
interaction \citep{bamberger2025measuring,kemper2025message}; weighted Jacobi
carries the same reading, each sweep extending the iterate's nonzero region
along adjacent edges, so a refined mesh needs more sweeps to propagate a source
\citep{moore2025gnnla}.
Proposition~\ref{prop:reach} instead fixes this experiment's source-generated
access class and turns support into an instance-specific floor; it is narrower
than general matrix-vector query lower bounds
\citep{derezinski2026matrixvector}. Learned multigrid, UGrid, ARMA filters,
and graph-neural preconditioners fall outside the source-generated access class
\citep{greenfeld2019multigrid,luz2020amg,han2024ugrid,bianchi2022arma,
chen2025gnp}.
Their nonlocal setup and application work must be counted separately.

\paragraph{Structured computation as inductive bias.}
Optimization and declarative layers embed structured solution operators inside
trainable networks and are motivated as inductive biases
\citep{amos2017optnet,agrawal2019differentiable,gould2022deep,paulus2024lpgd}.
The question here is narrower: whether the numerical exactness of such an
operator adds a further generalization bias once the competing realizations
attain comparable training fit.

\paragraph{Approximate implicit differentiation.}
Iteration-complexity analyses, inconsistent partial-solve adjoints, and
differentiable preconditioned Krylov methods already frame approximate implicit
gradients \citep{grazzi2020iteration,akbarzadeh2020consistent,montoison2026differentiable}.
Jacobian-free backpropagation, phantom gradients, Broyden reuse, and implicit
language models report successful inexact training
\citep{fung2022jfb,geng2021training,nguyen2023efficient,schone2025implicit}.
PRDP trains through deliberately under-converged iterative sparse linear solves,
under both unrolled and implicit differentiation, and still reaches full network
accuracy \citep{bhatia2025prdp}. In bilevel learning the hypergradient rule can
matter at least as much as the lower-level solver \citep{ehrhardt2024analyzing},
the same rule sensitivity the GMRES adversary finds here.
One deployed DEQ compares them directly and keeps the inexact estimator, which
reaches a similar score and cuts training from $56$ hours to $32$
\citep{zheng2023deqnar}.
Reversible DEQs report benefits from exact gradients through another
construction \citep{mccallum2025reversible}. Successful inexact training
elsewhere already rules out a universal exact-gradient advantage. The
confirmatory result is Neumann-specific, and the GMRES adversary makes that
boundary empirical.
