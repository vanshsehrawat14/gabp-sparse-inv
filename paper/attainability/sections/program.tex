\section{Limits and questions left open}
\label{sec:program}

The two preregistered probes resolve to attainability against the tested
truncations, not inductive bias among equally fit models. GMRES narrows the DEQ
half again: eight products suffice in that exploratory population.

\paragraph{What is established.}
The hard cells never supply the equal-fit comparison required by the
hypothesis. The routed-field certificate extends this ruling to its
source-generated class. In DEQ, GMRES reaches the exact arm's learned function
in the median where matched-product Neumann does not, establishing rule
sensitivity rather than a general finite-budget barrier. The fp32 sweep gives no robust accuracy
advantage over a well-ordered dense factorization.

\paragraph{What is not established.}
The experiments do not show that sparse direct is uniformly better than
iterative methods, helps on targets not shaped by the operator, or has
condition-independent error. They do not rule out a different architecture,
optimizer, preconditioner, or multiscale solver. The implementation is a
reference, not a systems competitor.

\paragraph{Questions left open.}
Selected uncertainty needs matched-time and matched-memory comparisons against
conjugate gradients and stochastic Lanczos quadrature
\citep{gardner2018gpytorch,ubaru2017fast}; variational or Vecchia methods change
the statistical model \citep{hensman2013gaussian,katzfuss2021general}. DEQ
benchmark claims likewise need preconditioned solvers, phantom gradients, and
accelerated fixed-point methods
\citep{geng2021training,anderson1965iterative,broyden1965class}. The present
GMRES arm is unpreconditioned and confined to a scalar-gain chain.

\paragraph{Evidence and structural boundary.}
The failed DEQ control, ambiguous maze floor, and excluded reused-seed branch
remain part of the ruling (Appendix~\ref{app:integrity}). Exact sparse direct is
useful only while elimination fronts remain manageable and reuse justifies
factorization. If an accurate iterative method is cheaper, exactness is simply
unnecessary.
