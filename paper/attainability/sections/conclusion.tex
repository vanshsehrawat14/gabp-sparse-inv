\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Selected inversion is classical; here it provides the exact structured
intervention. In both preregistered 30-seed probes, the finite Jacobi or
Neumann arm fails to attain comparable fit in the hard cells. The equal-fit
inductive-bias comparison is therefore absent. The failed DEQ control,
conflicting maze diagnostics, and excluded reused-seed branch keep that ruling
hedged. Proposition~\ref{prop:reach} nevertheless certifies the routed-field
result for its source-generated class: at seven products, its test-error floor
is $578$ times the exact arm's error.

The later GMRES falsifier makes the DEQ conclusion a lot more specific. At eight
matched products, GMRES clears the comparable-fit bar in most seeds and reaches
the exact arm's learned function in the median; Neumann does neither. The first-read ruling remains valid
for its frozen Neumann arms, but finite access itself does not explain the gap
at this budget; at four products and below, joint non-attainment remains
unresolved.

These are CPU-scale, low-treewidth, solve-shaped tasks. The evidence does not
support the initial inductive-bias explanation or a performance claim against
preconditioned or multiscale solvers. Those questions need new protocols
(Section~\ref{sec:program}).
